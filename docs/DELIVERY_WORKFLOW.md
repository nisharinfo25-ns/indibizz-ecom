# IndiaBizz COD & Logistics Operational Workflow

This document explains the end-to-end lifecycle of Cash on Delivery (COD) commerce and logistics within IndiaBizz.

---

## 1. Complete Order to Cash Lifecycle

```
[ Customer Placed Order (COD) ]
              │
              ▼
[ Owner Approval ] ─── Reject ──► [ Stock Released ]
              │
          Approved
              ▼
[ Packing & Label Generation ]
              │
          Ready for Pickup
              ▼
[ Pickup Assignment (Own Fleet / Partner) ]
              │
          Accept Assignment
              ▼
[ Staff Arrived at Seller Hub ]
              │
   Verify Pickup OTP (from Seller)
              ▼
[ In Transit / Arrived at Town Destination ]
              │
      Start Out For Delivery
              │
   (SMS with 6-digit OTP sent to Customer)
              ▼
[ Staff Arrived at Customer Doorstep ]
              │
   Collect Physical Cash ◄── (Paise exact balance)
              │
   Verify Delivery OTP (from Customer)
              │
   (Optional: Capture Photo Proof of Delivery)
              ▼
[ Status -> DELIVERED ]
   - Cash entered into Staff Custody Ledger
   - Order closed for delivery SLA
              │
              ▼
[ End of Shift: Cash Handover Declaration ]
              │
   Cashier / Hub Manager Verifies Cash & Approves
              │
[ Status -> SETTLED ]
```

---

## 2. Key Safeguards & Business Rules

1. **Dual OTP Protection**:
   - **Pickup Handover**: Delivery staff cannot pick up packed orders without entering the secret 6-digit OTP generated by the seller.
   - **Delivery Handover**: Delivery staff cannot complete delivery or mark status as DELIVERED without entering the 6-digit OTP sent to the customer's verified phone number.

2. **Immutable Financial Accounting**:
   - Cash collected in the field is logged to `cod_settlements` and tied to the specific staff member's custody.
   - Handover must be declared by the driver and counter-verified by the hub cashier.
   - Surplus or shortage is calculated down to the single Paisa.

3. **Cancellations & Returns**:
   - Orders can only be cancelled by customers prior to driver pickup.
   - Once delivered, customer can request a doorstep return with photo evidence.
   - Return pickups undergo reverse verification before refund or store credit is approved.
